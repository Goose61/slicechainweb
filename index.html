03 · About SlicePay
About SlicePay

Built for businesses that want to move faster.

We created SlicePay - the Slice Chain flagship crypto payments processor from SliceChain Holdings - to help merchants accept cryptocurrency worldwide: QR and POS in person, hosted checkout online, and USDC settlement without replacing existing workflows.

I.

Scan & Pay

Customers scan your SlicePay QR code crypto payment and pay from any compatible wallet to accept cryptocurrency payments at checkout.

II.

SLICE Loyalty

Buyers receive SLICE tokens after each purchase and redeem them for discounts on future orders.

III.

Staff incentives

Employees who facilitate a transaction earn 0.3% of the 1.9% platform fee, paid in supported stablecoins.

IV.

Owner payouts

Business owners receive USDC and USDT payouts with transparent pricing and no hidden settlement delays.

V.

Multi-chain support

Accept payments across Ethereum, Solana, Monad, Stellar, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, HyperEVM, BNB Chain, and Linea from a single QR flow.

VI.

Online checkout

Add hosted crypto checkout to any website with one embed script or API-created invoice. No iframe, no wallet SDK, and no blockchain code on your store.
